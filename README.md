# MovieFinder Website

Created using React and Next framework

## Description:

MovieFinder is a mobile app designed to enhance your movie-watching experience. Whether you're looking for your next favorite film or organizing your watchlist, we've got you covered. App aims to provide a new intuitive method to browse movies. Combining modern trend of short-form content and infinite scroll to improve movie browsing experience. Providing an engaging yet informative experience as opposed to modern streaming platforms.

## Website purpose:

Showcase and offer APK for app. Also show the development timeline of the app.
